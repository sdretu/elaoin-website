Privacy Policy — Tictic Metronome

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Overview

Metronome is a simple, standalone utility app. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with respect to your privacy.

<!--more-->

## Data Collection

Metronome collects no data of any kind.

Specifically, the app does not:

• Collect personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
• Use analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services
• Transmit any data to external servers
• Use advertising networks or tracking identifiers
• Access your microphone, camera, contacts, or any other sensitive capability
• Create user accounts or require sign-in

All state (BPM, tempo settings) is kept locally on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Apple Platform Features

The app uses the following Apple platform features, all processed entirely on your device:

• Live Activity / Dynamic Island (iOS) — displays BPM on the Lock Screen; no data leaves the device
• Haptic feedback (Apple Watch) — generates wrist vibrations locally; no data is involved
• Control Center widget — provides quick access; no data is collected

## Third-Party Services

Metronome uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or services. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertisements.

## Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app ever introduces features that involve data, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and users will be notified via an App Store update.
